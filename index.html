About

Technical work with a policy and infrastructure lens.

I am a computer science student at MIT interested in how data, software, and AI can make large systems more understandable and more useful.

A recurring theme in my work is turning messy operational or research data into something people can actually use: a reproducible workflow, an interpretable model, a decision-support interface, or a cleaner story for non-technical teams.

Most of my recent work has centered on energy systems, distributed energy resources, transportation sentiment analysis, and technical tooling that helps people explore patterns rather than just collect outputs.
